## STEP 8 - LOCO (backbone panel, 19 markers): expr-only vs expr+spatial

Run on Kaggle (300k/cohort train cap, full held-out cohort for test). Permutation control
shuffles the spatial block in the test set to check the spatial gain is real signal, not leakage.

| held_out | n_train | n_test_L1 | expr F1_L1 | expr+spatial F1_L1 | permuted F1_L1 | dF1 (spatial) |
|:---------|--------:|----------:|-----------:|--------------------:|---------------:|---------------:|
| CRC      |  772221 |    207862 |     0.5704 |               0.6039 |         0.5722 |        +0.0335 |
| HubMap   |  677458 |    842756 |     0.7094 |               0.7265 |         0.7026 |        +0.0171 |
| Keren    |  826131 |    163690 |     0.6509 |               0.6335 |         0.6091 |        -0.0174 |
| UPMC     |  677458 |   1379492 |     0.7742 |               0.7833 |         0.7446 |        +0.0091 |
| ferguson |  956564 |    149604 |     0.3849 |               0.3918 |         0.3850 |        +0.0069 |

**Reading:** spatial context helps cross-cohort transfer for CRC/HubMap/UPMC/ferguson
(+0.007 to +0.034 F1_L1), and for CRC/HubMap/ferguson the permuted score falls back close to
the expr-only baseline — confirming the gain is genuinely spatial, not a leak. Keren is the
one cohort where adding spatial features *hurts* (-0.017); its spatial block doesn't transfer
well onto held-out Keren.
